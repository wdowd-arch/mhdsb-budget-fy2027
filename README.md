# The Marblehead Independent
## FY2027 Select Board budget dashboard

**An interactive breakdown of Marblehead's $56.6 million fiscal year 2027 budget, approved by the Select Board on March 19, 2026.**

This dashboard was built to accompany original reporting by The Marblehead Independent on the town's budget crisis. It allows readers to explore what's actually inside the budget — department by department, line item by line item — rather than relying on top-line figures alone.

### What it does

- **Year-over-year comparison chart** showing FY2026 vs. FY2027 across all nine budget categories
- **Expandable accordion** with every department nested under its parent category
- **Hover tooltips** surfacing the specific line-item cuts, eliminated positions and new additions driving each department's change
- **AP style throughout**, with all dollar figures, position titles and terminology following Associated Press conventions

### Why it exists

The town's budget is up $4.8 million. Nearly all of that increase is consumed by health insurance ($16.75 million after an 11.17 percent rate hike), debt service ($11.1 million) and pension obligations that won't level off until 2036. What remains for discretionary services has been cut to the muscle.

The numbers tell one story. The hearing told another. This dashboard bridges the two.

- Responsive down to mobile
